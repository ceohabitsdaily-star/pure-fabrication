What We Offer

Full-scope ACP services
for your project.

01

Panel Supply & Fabrication

Custom-cut aluminum composite panels to spec — PE core for standard applications, FR/ASTM E84 fire-rated core for Ontario commercial projects, and A2 non-combustible core for high-rise above 18m.

02

Installation

Full facade installation including subframing, panel attachment, reveals, and finishing. We work to your schedule and coordinate directly with your site super.

03

Design Assist & Shop Drawings

We can support your team with panel layout drawings, reveal detailing, and shop drawings for permit and coordination — reducing back-and-forth with the architect.

04

Supply Only

Need panels fabricated to your dimensions and delivered to site? We supply custom-cut ACP panels in any finish — matte, gloss, brushed, wood grain, stone grain — with short lead times.
