If you're pricing an aluminum composite panel facade for a Toronto building, the first question is always the same: what's this going to cost? ACP cladding installed in the GTA ranges from roughly $28 to $90 per square foot — depending on the system type, panel specification, and complexity of the building envelope. This guide breaks that number down so you know exactly what you're paying for.

The Quick Answer

For a straightforward commercial storefront or residential re-clad with a direct-fix ACP system, expect $28–$45 per square foot installed (material + labour, no scaffold premium). A rainscreen system on a mid-rise commercial building with aluminum extrusion framing and thermally broken anchors runs $45–$70 per square foot. Complex architectural work — custom reveals, full-height corners, bespoke panel shapes — starts at $70 and can exceed $90 per square foot on premium projects.

These are supply-and-install figures for the GTA in 2026, including materials, fabrication, and installation labour. They do not include scaffolding or swing stage rental, which is quoted separately based on building height and site access.

What's Driving That Number

1. Panel Specification

Not all ACP is the same. The cost difference between a standard PE-core panel and an FR or A2 mineral-core panel can be $3–$8 per square foot in material cost alone. For buildings subject to Ontario Building Code fire requirements — anything above 3 storeys for most occupancy types — an FR or A2 core is not optional.

Finish matters too. Standard PVDF colour panels in stock colours are the most economical. Custom RAL colours, brushed metallic finishes, and specialty coatings (mirror, anodized-look) carry a 10–25% premium on the panel material cost.

2. Substructure System

The aluminum subframe that holds your panels to the building wall is often as expensive as the panels themselves. A basic direct-fix with Z-clips on a flat masonry wall is the lowest cost approach. A full engineered rainscreen with cold-formed aluminum hat channels, thermal spacers, and adjustable anchors into an IMP or sheathed backup wall is a significantly different scope — and price.

3. Building Height and Site Access

Labour pricing assumes standard scaffold access up to 4 storeys. For buildings requiring a swing stage, powered mobile scaffold, or aerial work platforms on busy Toronto streets, expect a 15–30% premium on installation labour, depending on site logistics and access restrictions.

4. Complexity of the Envelope

The number of outside corners, inside corners, window jamb returns, soffit transitions, and feature reveals all add linear footage to the installation. A simple box-shaped building with four flat walls is much less labour-intensive than a building with multiple setbacks, column reveals, and curtain wall interfaces.

Cost by System Type

The system you specify is the single biggest variable in your project budget. Here's what each one costs in the GTA and when it's the right choice.

Direct-Fix ACP — Simplest

Panels attach to the wall framing with Z-clips or similar concealed fasteners, directly to a substrate of wood, steel stud, or masonry. Best suited for low-rise residential, single-storey commercial, and small renovation projects. Lower material cost and fastest installation — but no ventilated cavity, so moisture management depends heavily on the backup wall detail.

$28–$45
per sq ft installed

Rainscreen System — Most Common for Commercial

The standard for commercial facade work in Ontario. An aluminum extrusion subframe creates a ventilated cavity behind the panels, allowing moisture to drain and the wall assembly to breathe. Thermally broken anchors prevent condensation at the structural connection. This system is specified by architects on the vast majority of GTA commercial projects over two storeys.

$45–$70
per sq ft installed

Cassette / Clip System — Architectural Grade

Panels are folded into cassettes that click into a horizontal track system. Individual panels can be removed without disturbing adjacent panels — ideal for high-traffic commercial buildings where future access is a consideration. Cleanest sight lines, premium appearance, and highest labour content. Typically specified on flagship retail, corporate headquarters, and high-end residential.

$62–$90+
per sq ft installed

ACP vs Other Cladding Materials

ACP is not always the cheapest option on a per-square-foot basis — but the comparison changes significantly over a 20-year window.

Material GTA Installed Cost (2026) Maintenance Typical Finish Life
ACP Cladding $28–$90 / sq ft Minimal — wash annually 30–40 years (PVDF)
Brick / Masonry $35–$75 / sq ft Repointing every 15–20 yrs 50+ years structure
Stucco / EIFS $18–$40 / sq ft Crack sealing every 5–10 yrs 15–25 years
Fiber Cement $22–$50 / sq ft Repainting every 5–10 yrs 20–30 years
Solid Aluminum Plate $70–$130 / sq ft Minimal 40+ years

ACP's value proposition isn't always the lowest first cost — it's the 30-year PVDF finish warranty, zero repainting, and the ability to match exact brand or design colours consistently across every panel. For commercial buildings where brand identity and maintenance cost matter, ACP nearly always wins the life-cycle comparison.
